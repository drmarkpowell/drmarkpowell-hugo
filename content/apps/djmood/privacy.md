# DJ Mood Privacy Policy
This policy applies to all information collected or submitted on DJ Mood's apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and any other devices and platforms.

## Summary

**DJ Mood does not collect personal information and does not track you.**

## What Stays With You

Your settings and your Recently Played list stay with you. Recently Played syncs through your own iCloud account, which we cannot see.

## What the App Sends Over the Network

- **Playlist updates:** To stay current, the app downloads its playlist list from a server we operate. Like any web request, this shows your IP address to that server and its hosting provider. It contains no account information or device identifier.
- **Tips:** Tips are optional. They are processed by Apple, and we use RevenueCat to handle the purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and the purchase details, along with your IP address as with any web request. We do not receive or store your payment information.
- **Apple Music:** Playback and your library are handled by Apple Music under Apple's privacy policy.

We do not:

- Collect personal information
- Track your activity or share it for advertising
- Sell your data
- Use advertising or analytics services

## App Intents and Shortcuts

DJ Mood supports Siri, Spotlight, and Shortcuts integration. These features are processed locally on your device and follow Apple's privacy standards for App Intents.

## Security

All network transfers in the DJ Mood app use HTTPS to keep your information and use of the app secure.

## California Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance

We comply with the California Online Privacy Protection Act. We therefore will not distribute your personal information to outside parties without your consent. (We collect no personal information anyway.)

## Children's Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance

We never collect or maintain information at our website from those we actually know are under 13, and no part of our website is structured to attract anyone under 13. Since DJ Mood does not collect personal information, the app is safe for users of all ages who have access to Apple Music.

## Information for European Union Customers

Information may be processed, stored, and used outside of the country in which you are located. Data privacy laws vary across jurisdictions, and different laws may be applicable to your data depending on where it is processed, stored, or used.

## Your Rights

Since DJ Mood does not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete. Your Recently Played list can be managed through your iCloud settings.

## Your Consent

By using our site or apps, you consent to our privacy policy.

## Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store or via the MacStories Discord community.

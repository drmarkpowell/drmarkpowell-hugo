# DJ Mood Press Kit

## TL;DR

DJ Mood unlocks over 250 hidden Apple Music playlists that you can only discover by asking Siri — until now. All beautifully organized by mood, activity, and vibe.

## What is DJ Mood?

DJ Mood surfaces Apple Music's "Siri playlists" — 250+ mood and activity-based playlists that Apple created but never made browsable. These playlists are normally only discoverable by asking Siri things like "play something happy" or "play workout music." DJ Mood presents them all in a beautiful, organized interface so you can browse and discover without guessing what to ask.

Users will enjoy:

- 250+ curated Apple Music playlists organized by mood, activity, and vibe
- Beautiful emoji-based navigation that makes discovery fun
- A delightful, swipe-friendly interface on iPhone
- A powerful sidebar experience on iPad and Mac
- A gorgeous full-screen experience on Apple TV
- Effortless one-tap playback — just tap a mood and let the music play
- Comprehensive VoiceOver accessibility support

## Who is DJ Mood for?

DJ Mood is for anyone with an Apple Music subscription who wants to discover new music based on how they're feeling. It's perfect for:

- People who never knew Apple Music had hundreds of hidden mood-based playlists
- Anyone tired of searching for the "right" playlist for their current vibe
- Music lovers who want to explore playlists for activities like working out, focusing, relaxing, or celebrating
- Apple TV users who want a beautiful music discovery experience on the big screen
- Anyone who appreciates thoughtfully designed, privacy-respecting apps

## Key Features

**Curated by Apple, surfaced by DJ Mood**
Access 250+ "Siri playlists" that Apple created but never made browsable — from "Feeling Happy" to "Rainy Day Vibes" to "Workout Intensity."

**Beautiful on every device**
Enjoy a delightful, swipe-friendly interface on iPhone, a powerful sidebar on iPad and Mac, and a gorgeous full-screen experience on Apple TV.

**Effortless discovery**
No more guessing what to ask Siri or hunting through search. Just open the app, tap a mood, and let the music play.

**Fully accessible**
Comprehensive VoiceOver support ensures everyone can navigate by mood and discover great music.
